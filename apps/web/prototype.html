FAO Poultry Disease Manual · Chapter 4 (Coccidiosis) 0.94 Similarity
Eimeria tenella causes severe caecal coccidiosis characterized by bleeding into the caecal pouches, pale combs, and high mortality in birds 3 to 6 weeks old. Fresh bloody droppings are the primary pathognomonic clinical sign.
NVRI Vom Guidelines · Avian Enteritis Protocol 0.88 Similarity
Differential diagnosis: Differentiate bloody coccidiosis from necrotic enteritis. Necrotic enteritis produces dark, watery, foul-smelling droppings without bright frank blood. Separate all symptomatic birds immediately.
